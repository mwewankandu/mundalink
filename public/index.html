Market prices

Each report shows its source and date. Demo only means there is no verified kwacha amount yet. MundaLink will not invent one. Official purchase prices, such as FRA, are labelled separately from open-market prices.

Later, real figures can come from the Ministry of Agriculture, ZAMACE, ZamStats, and ZARI post-harvest notes.

Waste less

Short habits that cut post-harvest loss. For a serious pest, disease, or food-safety problem, ask an agricultural extension officer.

  • Harvest gently. Pick in the cooler hours and leave bruised fruit out of the bag you will sell.
  • Sort and grade. Keep similar size and ripeness together so buyers can see what they are paying for.
  • Pack clean. Use crates or bags that can breathe. Do not seal tomatoes in plastic in the sun.
  • Store maize dry. Dry it well, keep it off the soil, and protect it from rats and rain.
